Our story

From styling South Africa's stages to Minner Street

Pre_Luxx began with a single rail of curated eveningwear — it's grown into a full atelier, still built around one client at a time.

Where we started

2016: a single rail, one fitting room

Pre_Luxx studied fashion design at the Cape Peninsula University of Technology before spending years as a stylist, dressing clients for weddings, award shows, and matric dances across the Western Cape. In 2016, she opened Pre_Luxx Boutique on Mineer Street — a single curated rail of eveningwear and one fitting room.

A decade of styling experience meant she knew exactly what clients struggled to find: pieces that felt tailored, not rented; personal, not off-the-rack. That idea is still what Pre_Luxx is built around today.
